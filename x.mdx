Take advantage of X's Filtered Stream product without writing code or managing complex HTTP stream connections. We connect to X's filtered stream, manage disconnects, and deliver matches to your webhook in near real-time.

## Latency
X delivers matches to XSDR within 6-7 seconds of P99 latency. We immediately forward those matches to your webhook.

## Rule Syntax
When building rules, X supports standalone operators and conjunction-required operators. Operators are used to build robust filters for your requirements. Refer to X's [documentation](https://docs.x.com/x-api/posts/filtered-stream/integrate/operators) for a full list of supported operators.
